# Pricing and unit economics
All amounts in GHS; no published price approved.
Direct cost C = delivery hours × internal hourly cost + external costs + allocated delivery overhead + contingency.
Minimum price before applicable taxes = C / (1 - target gross margin), where 0 <= margin < 1.
Illustration only: 10 hours × GHS 40 + GHS 100 external cost + GHS 50 contingency = GHS 550; at 30% target margin, price floor GHS 785.72 rounded upward to pesewa. Not a market quote.
Contribution = net revenue - variable delivery cost. Break-even jobs = ceiling(monthly fixed cost / positive contribution per job).
Quote checklist: scope, exclusions, acceptance criteria, delivery milestones, payment terms, validity, taxes where applicable, support and change control. Record actual hours and costs after delivery.
Do not confuse margin with markup; do not assume VAT registration or tax rates. Confirm applicable Ghana tax treatment with qualified adviser.
