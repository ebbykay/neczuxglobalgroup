# 30-day commercial validation plan
Proposed start: 25 November 2026. Owner approval pending.
Days 1–7: identify 10 relevant businesses, conduct 3 discovery conversations, document pain and current cost. Targets, not achieved results.
Days 8–14: select one repeatable problem, define pilot scope and cost floor, seek two qualified quote opportunities.
Days 15–21: pursue one paid pilot; deliver only if agreement and capacity permit.
Days 22–30: review actual cost, contribution, customer acceptance and repeatability; decide continue, pivot or stop.
Success criteria: evidence of willingness to pay, positive expected contribution, feasible delivery, and at least one documented acceptance if a pilot is sold. No fabricated leads or outreach performed by this document.
Owner decisions: available weekly hours, internal hourly cost, monthly overhead, target margin, preferred initial segment, approved payment terms.
