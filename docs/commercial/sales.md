# Sales playbook
1. Identify a business with observable operational friction; use lawful business contact channels.
2. Discovery: current process, frequency, error/rework, decision owner, budget, urgency, data constraints.
3. Qualify: problem, authority, budget and delivery feasibility. Disqualify if no measurable need or capacity.
4. Offer a small paid pilot with clear deliverable and acceptance test; do not perform full implementation for free.
5. Written quote and agreement before work; confirm payment terms and scope.
6. Deliver, obtain acceptance, invoice, reconcile actual settlement and offer support.
7. Review contribution and client feedback; request permission before publishing case studies.
Metrics: qualified opportunities, accepted quotes / decided quotes, collected revenue, contribution, delivery hours, repeat business. Track denominators and period.
No unsolicited bulk messaging, fabricated testimonials or confidential data in public GitHub.
