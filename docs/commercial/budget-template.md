# Monthly budget template (complete privately)
Month | Opening cash | Opening receivables | Billed revenue | Variable cost | Fixed cost | Capital expenditure | Other cash in | Other cash out | Same-month collection fraction
Record assumption source, owner, date, scenario, actual result and variance. Separate personal and corporate funds. Do not double-count allocated overhead, taxes or transfers. Review cash shortfalls before commitments; no automatic financing or expenditure authority.
