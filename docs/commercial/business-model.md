# Business model
Initial target hypotheses: owner-managed SMEs, professional service firms, and organisations with repetitive spreadsheet/reporting workflows in Ghana. These are hypotheses, not validated demand.
Offer outcome-based paid pilots: workflow diagnosis and automation; management reporting; website and inquiry conversion; business process documentation. Avoid regulated lending decisions, investment advice and handling client funds without appropriate approvals.
Revenue: fixed-scope implementation, recurring support, paid training, later software licensing. Procurement only on verified supplier quote and written client approval.
Selection criteria: measurable pain, decision-maker access, ability to pay, repeatability, delivery capacity, data sensitivity.
Do not promise results without baseline evidence. No discounts or commitments without owner approval.
