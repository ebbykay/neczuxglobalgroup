# Financial planning v0.1
Monthly deterministic projections in GHS; no historical data or approved budget. Inputs: opening cash, opening receivables, monthly billed revenue, variable and fixed cash costs, capital expenditure, other cash inflows/outflows, same-month collection fraction. Remaining billed revenue and all opening receivables assumed collected in the following projection month (opening receivables in month one). No bad debt, multi-month ageing, tax, accrual accounting, depreciation, debt schedules or financing optimisation. Operating surplus is billed revenue less specified operating costs, not audited profit. Negative cash indicates an unfunded gap, not an available overdraft. Scenario rates are illustrative and not probabilities.
Requirements FP01 cash reconciliation, FP02 collection timing, FP03 funding gap, FP04 sensitivity, FP05 input validation. Verified by tests/test_planning.py; run python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_*.py' -v. Keep actual budgets and forecasts private; owner must approve assumptions. Statistical forecasting requires historical data, backtesting and uncertainty assessment.
