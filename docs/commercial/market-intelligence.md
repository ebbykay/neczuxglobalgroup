# Market intelligence and opportunity triage
Status: provisional hypotheses, not market research findings.
Initial segments: owner-managed service SMEs, distributors with spreadsheet workflows, and professional firms with reporting bottlenecks. Validate through interviews and public business information; no fabricated leads.
Criteria: problem severity 25%, budget 25%, authority 15%, urgency 10%, delivery fit 15%, repeatability 10%. Each 0–5 rating requires a documented basis; 0 means confirmed absent, 5 strong verified evidence, missing means unknown. Missing criteria produce lower/upper score bounds and research status, not an assumed zero. Legal and delivery feasibility gates override scores; low budget or authority requires qualification. Thresholds 75/50 are proposed, not calibrated. No sensitive personal attributes.
Research protocol: record source, date, claim, confidence, competing explanation and next validation step. Verify decision-maker and willingness to pay through consent-based discovery. No unsolicited bulk outreach, scraping private records, or automatic rejection. Store real prospect records privately. Review scoring against observed conversion and contribution after sufficient data; do not call it probability of purchase.
