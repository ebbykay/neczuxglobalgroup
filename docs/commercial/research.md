# Research and product pipeline
Research themes: business process automation, applied analytics, optimisation, credit risk and decision support.
Stage gates: problem evidence -> literature and prior-art review -> data rights and feasibility -> prototype -> reproducible evaluation -> pilot -> commercial decision.
First candidate: SME reporting and workflow automation toolkit; validate demand before building. Credit risk intelligence remains a separate candidate subject to academic IP rights, data licensing, local validation, model governance and human decision oversight.
Each experiment records hypothesis, sources, dataset provenance, methods, metrics, limitations, reproducibility instructions and decision.
Do not publish customer data, proprietary research or credentials in this public repository. Use a private research workspace for sensitive work.
