# Immediate client operations

Do not store customer records in this public repository. Use a restricted business inbox and private lead register.

## Intake
1. Monitor the existing Formspree destination and WhatsApp business number. Confirm both are controlled by Neczux before launch.
2. Assign a unique lead ID and record date, contact, division, request type, requirements, budget, owner, next action and status.
3. Acknowledge receipt; do not promise availability or delivery before review.
4. Qualify scope, budget, timing, capacity and dependencies. For procurement verify supplier stock, warranty and delivery.
5. Issue a written quotation with scope, exclusions, price/currency, validity, milestones and payment terms. Obtain written acceptance before work.
6. Confirm payment instructions privately; verify funds independently. Never request card credentials.
7. Track delivery, acceptance, invoice and follow-up.

Statuses: New, Contacted, Qualified, Quoted, Accepted, In delivery, Completed, Closed/lost.

## Release gate
Owner must verify form destination with a labelled test enquiry, WhatsApp ownership, business email, privacy notice, actual service capacity and payment instructions. Static CI does not establish delivery. No production merge until release approval and checks. No real customer records in GitHub.

## Acknowledgement template
Thank you for contacting Neczux. We have received your enquiry and will review your requirements. We will confirm scope, availability, pricing and next steps before any commitment.

## Quote template
Reference; client; date; scope; deliverables; exclusions; price and currency; taxes if applicable; payment schedule; estimated timeline; validity; acceptance; contact.
