# Neczux enterprise roadmap

Strategic direction: Technology, intelligence, research and business development.

1. Website audit and remediation — reliable corporate website.
2. Business positioning and service packaging — clear, sellable offerings.
3. Neczux Operating System — internal business management infrastructure.
4. Customer management and automation — structured commercial operations.
5. Proprietary software products — scalable revenue opportunities.
6. Research and innovation — defensible intellectual property.
7. Enterprise expansion — specialised business divisions.

## Next milestone: Neczux OS
Build a private internal application separate from the public website. Start with authenticated access, role-based authorisation, lead and client records, quotation lifecycle, engagements, tasks, invoices and payment status. Use manual verified settlement before payment integration. Add audit history, backups, export and tests. No real client records, secrets or proprietary research in this public repository. Choose hosting and database after reviewing cost, privacy and operational constraints; no paid commitment without approval.

## Release gates
Demonstrate lead-to-quotation-to-engagement-to-invoice workflow using synthetic data; verify access isolation, validation, auditability and backup restoration before accepting live records. Verify public form delivery independently. Prioritise operational value over cosmetic redesign.

## Later phases
Customer portal and consent-aware automation; proprietary products with validated demand; research governance, reproducibility and IP review; expand divisions based on demonstrated demand and capacity.
